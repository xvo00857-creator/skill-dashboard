## 2. Phase 3 — Critique

- The decision was **not wrong on the information we had** — it was wrong because (a) the information was thinner than we treated it, (b) execution missed its own gating dates, and (c) the external environment shifted in knowable-in-hindsight ways.
- We are not here to relitigate Feb 15. We are here to make sure the *next* market entry doesn't repeat the failure modes.
- The founder's override (5 cities → 2) reduced downside but also reduced surface area for learning; with 2 cities and no AE, we were under-resourced for the 2 we kept.

## 3. Phase 4 — Synthesis (proposed)

1. **Pause direct Spain expansion.** Convert to a partner-led motion for 6 months; revisit 2027-Q1 with real data.
2. **Supersede** the 2026-02-15 decision. Do not delete it.
3. **Reopen** the "local distributor/partner" question — but note this is a DO_NOT_RESURFACE item from Feb 15, so it requires explicit founder reauthorization per the skill's conflict rules.
4. **Add gating** to every future market entry: signed contracts (not LOIs) count as committed; Day-45 kill/continue review; CAC re-baseline at n≥30 signed.
5. **Rewire review discipline:** every decision's `Review:` date must produce a written 1-page check-in, or the action is auto-escalated.
