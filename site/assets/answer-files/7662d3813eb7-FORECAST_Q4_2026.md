## 1. Executive summary

The fact pattern — **fewer new logos, higher ACV** — is a textbook regime-change signal. The Skill's 70/30 blend (last-4Q weighted 70%, last-12Q weighted 30%) is built exactly for this: it pulls the forecast toward the recent softness without overfitting a single bad quarter (Tunguz canon).

On the encoded fact pattern, the Skill produces **two structural warnings** that should drive the board conversation regardless of the exact dollar number:

1. **Pipeline coverage is 2.72x — below the 3.0x SaaS floor** (Pacific Crest / KeyBanc). The commit number is structurally unsupported even if every conversion assumption is right.
2. **Two recent cohorts are leaking** — 2026-Q1 (NRR 6 pp below trailing) and 2026-Q2 (NRR 15.5 pp below trailing). The consolidated NRR still looks tolerable (96–98%) because older, larger cohorts dominate the weighted average. Per Campbell/Skok, the leak surfaces in the topline 2–3 quarters later — i.e., right about when the board is looking at Q1 2027.

The ACV rise is **a double-edged signal**: it props up the bookings number now, but (a) higher-ACV deals concentrate risk in fewer logos, (b) they carry longer sales cycles and net terms that stretch cash, and (c) if the ACV rise is *pricing-push* rather than *mix-shift-up*, the 2026-Q2 leaky cohort is the early warning of pricing-misalignment churn (Ramanujam canon).

## 3. Driving variables — what moves each scenario

### 3.1 New-logo volume (the #1 driver)

- **Encoded fact:** discovery-stage conversion last-4Q is 22% vs. 36% last-12Q — a **14-point drop, well above Tunguz's 10pp regime-change threshold**. The blend gives 26.2%.
- **Why it matters:** top-of-funnel is the only MEDIUM-confidence stage in the funnel (CoV 14.6%); every later stage is HIGH (CoV < 10%). That means the *uncertainty is concentrated where the slowdown is*, not in late-stage execution.
- **Scenario lever:** optimistic assumes early-stage conversion reverts ~halfway to 36%; baseline holds the blend; conservative holds 22%.

### 3.2 ACV / deal size

- **Encoded fact:** in-flight opps average ~$185K with several $250K–$420K deals; cohort starting ARR per logo has stepped up from ~$85K (2025) to ~$130K (2026-Q2 run-rate).
- **Why it matters:** higher ACV flatters the bookings number but (a) reduces logo count → concentration risk, (b) lengthens sales cycles (the $420K verbal and $340K contract_out are the two largest and carry the most timing risk), (c) often correlates with longer payment terms.
- **Scenario lever:** ACV holds across all three; the swing is *how many of the big ones close*, not whether ACV changes. If ACV starts compressing (discounting to win), that's a fourth scenario not modeled here — treat as downside.

## 4. Cash impact

Bookings are not cash. The translation uses standard SaaS billing assumptions (**replace with your actual terms**):

- **60%** of new bookings collected upfront in-quarter (annual prepay / multi-year deals)
- **40%** billed net-30 to net-60 → ~85% collected in the following quarter, ~15% slips to Q+2
- No financing / payment-plan deals modeled
- Existing-base churn/contraction from the leaky cohorts hits cash at renewal (mostly Q4 2026 – Q2 2027 for the 2026-Q1/Q2 cohorts)

| Scenario | Q4 bookings | Q4 cash collected | Q1 2027 spillover | Q4+Q1 cash |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | $0.85M | ~$0.51M | ~$0.29M | **~$0.80M** |
| Baseline | $1.04M | ~$0.63M | ~$0.35M | **~$0.98M** |
| Optimistic | $1.64M | ~$0.98M | ~$0.56M | **~$1.54M** |

**Cash-specific warnings:**

1. **The big deals drive cash timing.** The $420K verbal (OPP-305) and $340K contract_out (OPP-303) together are 43% of baseline commit. If either slips to Q1, Q4 cash drops ~$0.3–0.4M.
2. **Higher ACV often means longer net terms.** If the new $250K+ deals carry net-60 or milestone billing instead of upfront, the Q4 cash number is overstated by 10–20%. Confirm payment terms on the top 5 deals weekly.
3. **Leaky-cohort churn is a cash hit, not just an NRR hit.** The 2026-Q2 cohort's GRR decaying from 84% → 77% over four quarters implies ~$150–200K of annualized contraction/churn from that cohort alone as it hits renewal. That's a Q1–Q2 2027 cash drag, not a Q4 event, but it should be in the cash forecast now.
4. **Pipeline-coverage < 3x is also a cash-coverage problem.** Coverage is measured on pipeline $, but cash lags bookings by a quarter. If Q4 bookings come in at conservative ($0.85M), Q1 cash from new logos is ~$0.29M + whatever Q1 itself generates — plan the burn floor accordingly.

## 7. Assumptions and limits (non-optional block)

**Methodology assumptions (from the Skill, not negotiable):**

- Conversion is a **70/30 blend** of last-4Q and last-12Q windows. This captures regime change without overfitting one bad quarter (Tunguz + MIT Sloan/Hyndman).
- Commit includes only commit-grade stages (verbal, contract_out, commit, closed_won_pending). Best-case adds proposal/negotiation/demo_completed. Pipe-only includes everything.
- Time-to-close decays linearly: 1.0 inside the quarter, 0.7 within 30 days late, 0.5 within 60, 0.3 within 90, 0.15 beyond.
- Stalled opps (age > 2× median stage age AND last activity > 45 days) get ×0.5 in commit.
- Pipeline coverage floor is 3.0x (Pacific Crest / KeyBanc). Below it, commit is structurally unsupported.
- Cohorts are flagged leaky at ≥ 5pp mean-NRR gap vs. earlier cohorts (Campbell/ProfitWell).
- CoV bands: HIGH < 10%, MEDIUM 10–25%, LOW 25–50%, VERY LOW > 50%. Minimum 4 quarters of history for stable CoV.

**Modeling assumptions I made for the cash and scenario layer (replace with your actuals):**

- 60% upfront / 40% net-30-60 billing split on new bookings.
- Conservative scenario haircuts are named in §3.4; they are not generated by the script (the script produces one commit number; the conservative layer is a transparent overlay).
- No expansion bookings from existing customers are modeled in the bookings number (the cohort projector covers NRR/expansion separately).
- No SBC, no services revenue, no multi-year deal discounting.
- Target period is Q4 2026 (next full quarter as of 2026-08-10). If you need Q3 in-flight or Q1 2027, change `target_period` and re-run.

**What this forecast does not cover:**

- GAAP revenue recognition (that's `finance/financial-analysis`, not this Skill).
- Multi-year strategic planning / fundraise scenarios (that's `cfo-advisor`).
- Pricing changes (this Skill projects revenue *at* prices already set; if ACV rise is from a price change, run pricing analysis separately).
- Territory design, comp plans, sales leadership calls (that's `cro-advisor`).

**What would change the answer materially:**

- Real CRM pipeline export (the illustrative numbers are round and small — your real pipeline is almost certainly different in mix and size).
- Actual billing terms by deal (the 60/40 split is a placeholder).
- Actual cohort retention curves by segment (the 2026-Q2 leak is illustrative; your real cohort data may show a different shape — but if it shows *this* shape, the warning stands).
- Whether the ACV rise is upmarket mix-shift (healthy) vs. price-push on the same ICP (leaky-cohort risk). This is the single most important diagnostic question.
