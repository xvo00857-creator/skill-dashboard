## Assumptions to test

1. **"No acute effect → no chronic effect"** — Assumption, not evidence. Falsified by any good chronic exposure study showing harm.
2. **"Extrapolation from sub-6 GHz is valid"** — Assumption based on shared physics (non-ionizing, thermal mechanism). Falsified by consistent mmWave-specific effects at equal SAR.
3. **"Regulatory limits are protective"** — Based on thermal thresholds. Falsified by replicated non-thermal effects below limits.
4. **"Beamforming reduces exposure"** — Physically plausible (directed signals). Real-world measurement studies needed.
